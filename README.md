# Banking_Customer_segment_Analysis
Bank Customer Segmentation &amp; Credit Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

Problem Statement

The bank aims to understand customer behavior across different segments to:

Identify customers more likely to adopt credit products
Detect segments with high inactivity
Analyze which segments contribute the most to revenue (account balance)

Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Charts & Data Visualization

Key Insights
Middle-aged customers show the highest credit product adoption
Channel X3 is the most effective channel for credit products
Female customers have a higher inactivity rate
Entrepreneur and middle-aged customers contribute the highest account balance

Business Impact
Identified high-value customer segments for targeted marketing
Highlighted inactive segments for retention strategies
Provided actionable insights to improve revenue and customer engagement

Recommendations
Launch targeted credit campaigns for middle-aged customers
Improve underperforming channels using X3 as a benchmark
Design retention strategies for inactive female customers
Offer premium services to high-balance customer segments
